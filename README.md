## About Don Bosco AI
**Don Bosco AI ($BOSCO)** is a decentralized, post-quantum autonomous pedagogical intelligence and youth empowerment protocol inspired by the life, philosophy, and educational psychology of **Saint John Bosco (Don Bosco)** and the global Salesian movement.

The protocol translates Don Bosco’s world-renowned **Preventive System (*Il Sistema Preventivo*)** into Web4 decentralized architecture. By substituting punitive algorithms with empathetic active accompaniment (*assistenza*), digital oratories, zero-leakage vocational micro-apprenticeships, and quantum-resistant identity protection, Don Bosco AI forms *"Upright Citizens and Virtuous Souls"* (*Buoni cristiani e onesti cittadini*) among the world's youth.
